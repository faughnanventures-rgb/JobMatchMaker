Star a company to mark it as a favorite. Its jobs get a star and can be sorted to the top. Block a company to stop seeing its jobs.

Sites to check yourself

These employers' sites can't be read automatically, so their jobs will not show up on this dashboard. Open each one every week or two and look for yourself. Tap "I checked this" to keep track of when you last looked.

    Add or fix a company

    Paste a company's careers page link and check it. If the check can read jobs from it, save it to the watch list. To fix a company that could not be read, use its Fix link button above.

    Ask to add a company

    New companies are added by whoever maintains this site. Requests are saved in this browser, so copy the list and send it to them.
